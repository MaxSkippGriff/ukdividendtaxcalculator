About UKDividendTaxCalculator.co.uk

UKDividendTaxCalculator.co.uk is a free, independent tool for estimating UK dividend tax for the 2026/27 tax year. It is part of the UK Money Calculators network of free personal finance tools.

The calculator is designed for UK individuals receiving dividend income outside of ISAs — typically investors, limited company directors, and shareholders. It models the £500 dividend allowance, Personal Allowance tapering, and the three dividend tax rates (8.75%, 33.75%, 39.35%).

All calculations are estimates only. This tool is not a substitute for professional tax advice. For complex tax situations — including multiple income sources, trust income, foreign dividends, or Self Assessment queries — please consult a qualified accountant or tax adviser.

Editorial owner: James Whitfield, a UK personal finance writer with a background in dividend taxation, company director remuneration and investment income. James writes about the dividend allowance, dividend tax rates and self-assessment for investors and directors.

For corrections or feedback, email: editorial@ukdividendtaxcalculator.co.uk.
